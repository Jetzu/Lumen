# Consent to take part in a software usability study

**Study.** Comparative usability study of two image-analysis programs (Fiji/ImageJ and Lumen) on
everyday microscopy tasks.

**Organiser.** [name, group, institution, e-mail]

**What I am asked to do.** Complete ten short image-analysis tasks in each of two programs during
one session of about 75 minutes, think aloud while doing so, answer a ten-item questionnaire and
two open questions after each program, and give a few details about my experience (years of Fiji
use, scripting experience, operating system, screen size, input device, use of assistive
technology).

**Recording.** The screen of the study computer and my voice will be recorded. My face is not
recorded. The recording is labelled with a participant number, not with my name.

**Use of the data.** The recordings, logs and questionnaire answers are used to count task
completion, time and errors and to identify usability problems. Results are reported in aggregate
and, where single observations are quoted, without anything that identifies me. Short anonymised
quotations from the think-aloud may appear in the report. The organiser's key linking my
participant number to my name is kept separately and destroyed when the study ends
[or: on (date)]. Raw recordings are kept for [12 months] and then deleted; the anonymised
logs may be published with the study report.

**Voluntary participation.** Taking part is voluntary. I can stop at any time without giving a
reason and without any disadvantage. I can ask for my data to be deleted at any time up to the
publication of the results.

**Risks and benefits.** There are no risks beyond those of ordinary computer work. There is no
payment [or: a voucher of …]. The benefit is to the development of the software.

**Questions.** I can ask questions at any time before, during and after the session. [Contact for
questions and complaints.]
